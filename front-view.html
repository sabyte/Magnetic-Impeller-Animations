WHAT TO WATCH

The field is what the robot follows

In this parallel-axis geometry, the local field beneath the magnet rotates in the opposite sense. The robot follows it if the magnetic torque is sufficient. Both directions are compared from the right-hand ends of the shafts.

How to read this model

The field follows an ideal point-dipole calculation. The impeller is drawn following the field with a small illustrative lag; its dynamics are not simulated.

No measured RPM, field strength, or flow rate is implied.

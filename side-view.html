WHAT TO WATCH

A complete chain of motion

Motor rotation changes the field at the robot. Magnetic torque turns the secured internal magnets and impeller. The blades then drive fluid through the passage around the central hub.

How to read this model

The two cubes touch along the shaft; their N–S directions are across it, matching your corrected sketch. The hub is transparent when “Show magnets” is checked.

Right-to-left flow is illustrated for clockwise external rotation. Reversing rotation reverses the drawn flow. Actual pumping direction depends on blade handedness; flow rate is not calculated.
